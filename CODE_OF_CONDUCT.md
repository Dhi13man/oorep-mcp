# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a respectful, harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or
level of contribution.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and welcoming.
- Give and accept feedback with empathy.
- Focus disagreements on ideas and evidence, not people.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing another person's personal information.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository spaces and when representing the project in
public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to **<dhiman.seal@hotmail.com>**. Reports
will be reviewed fairly and kept confidential where practical. The maintainer
may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when necessary to protect the community.
